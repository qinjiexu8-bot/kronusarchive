# Kronus Archive Content Roadmap to 35 Indexable Articles

Updated: 2026-08-02

## Objective

Grow Kronus Archive from 14 currently indexable, source-checked articles to 35 indexable articles without publishing unverified gameplay numbers. Every pre-launch article must rely on a first-party Deep Silver, KING Art Games, Steam, or Games Workshop source. Hands-on claims remain reserved for launch-week testing.

## Publishing rules

- One primary search intent and one primary keyword per page.
- Cite at least two first-party sources when two are available.
- Mark every unsupported detail as TBA; never infer costs, percentages, timers, player limits, or build orders.
- Add three or four visible FAQs, matching FAQ JSON-LD, and two to five contextual internal links.
- Keep SEO titles near 50–60 characters including the brand suffix.
- Keep meta descriptions near 150–160 characters.
- Publish in batches of two to four, then validate sitemap, canonical, schema, lint, build, and layout.
- Restore an archived URL only after its article has been fully rewritten and rechecked.

## Launch-week replacement queue

These articles should not be published until repeatable hands-on evidence exists:

- Real minimum and recommended PC performance tests
- Verified Space Marines opening build order
- Verified Orks opening build order
- Verified Necrons opening build order
- Verified Adeptus Mechanicus opening build order
- Unit costs, production times, and tech-tree tables
- Commander ability and Wargear data
- Map-specific Annihilation, Domination, and Maelstrom strategy
- Last Stand hero, wave, progression, and team-composition guides

## Measurement checkpoints

Review Google Search Console after every four-article batch:

- Indexing status and discovered URLs
- Impressions and queries by content cluster
- Click-through rate by title pattern
- Cannibalization between pages targeting similar queries
- Internal-link coverage and orphan pages
- Pages with impressions but no clicks, which should be improved before adding more volume
